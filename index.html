Why the bot chose them

Best solve favours a word that can actually be the answer. Best information favours the guess that splits the remaining pool most evenly. Hybrid balances both. The controller usually switches to Best solve when the board is strongly constrained. But when several answers form a tight word family—especially a shared two-letter-or-longer ending—and a non-answer probe splits that family substantially better, it temporarily uses a Family probe instead of guessing family members one by one.
