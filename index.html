Why the bot chose them

Best solve favours a word that can actually be the answer. Best information favours the guess that splits the remaining pool most evenly. Hybrid balances both. The controller usually switches to Best solve when the board is strongly constrained. When the remaining answers form a tight family with two or more fixed positions—such as _ILLY, _IGHT, or __A_E—and a probe separates that family better, it temporarily uses a Family probe instead of guessing family members one by one.
This is a testing build. Batch archive mode automatically plays the bot against pasted historical answers; it does not control the live Wordle/Canuckle websites.
